## Funding Overview

### Funding, resource allocation, and the financial process for iGEM Toronto 2026.

We recognize that securing funding is one of the most significant challenges facing student-led scientific research projects. As a result, we treated fundraising as a core component of project planning from the outset. Early in the project cycle, we worked closely with sub-teams to identify anticipated resource requirements, developed a sponsorship package tailored to our project's goals, and conducted targeted outreach to both academic institutions and industry partners. Our objective was to establish a diversified funding base that could sustainably support the team while reducing reliance on any single source of financial support. Where possible, we also aligned our outreach efforts with institutional budgeting cycles, engaging prospective partners early to better understand their funding timelines and submit sponsorship requests ahead of key decision-making periods.

Resources secured through these efforts were strategically allocated according to team needs and project priorities. Funding was distributed across research and development activities, competition-related expenses, team operations, and outreach initiatives to ensure balanced support for all aspects of the project. Throughout the cycle, we maintained structured processes for budgeting, expense tracking, reimbursements, and financial record-keeping, promoting transparency, accountability, and responsible stewardship of team resources.

Collectively, these efforts formed an integral part of our broader operational strategy for the iGEM Toronto 2026 project cycle. By taking a proactive and long-term approach to financial planning, we helped ensure that the team remained in a stable and sustainable financial position, providing a strong foundation for future project cycles and continued growth.

## Financial Process

### Purchase approval

The Co-Presidents, Anthony Dinglasan and Purav Gupta, are the exclusive holders of the iGEM Toronto bank account, and every deposit and withdrawal requires their expressed permission regardless of which subteam or member is involved.

### Reimbursements

Members who pay out of pocket for an expense inside an approved budget are reimbursed, conditional on submitting receipts to the finance folder on the team drive. Each receipt filename is recorded against its transaction in the expense tracker, so every line item ties to supporting documentation. Reimbursements are issued by e-transfer and logged with the recipient, the vendor, the amount and the subteam. All receipts must be submitted by a deadline the presidents set after the Jamboree and before the final General Meeting of the cycle. Expenses submitted after that deadline are not covered, and extenuating circumstances raised beforehand are considered case by case. Jamboree ticket reimbursements run through a separate merit-based selection process, are paid only after a member attends in person, and are capped at one Team Member Ticket each.

### Reconciliation

Every transaction is logged in a central internal workbook with separate ledgers for revenue and expenses. Each expense records date, item, vendor, category, amount, units, funding source, subteam and notes. Expenses are classified into standard categories including COGS, OPEX, R&D, G&A, S&M, PPE and Other, which lets us analyze spending by function as well as by subteam.

The funding source field distinguishes cash from the team account against purchases made on an advisor's card, so each pool reconciles independently. Revenue is tracked as either cash or in-kind, which keeps compute credits such as our AWS and Azure awards separate from the cash position. A monthly summary rolls spending up by category to track burn across the cycle.

Financial information, including the account balance and the full record of inflows and outflows, is accessible to every team member at any time and kept current. The finance team prepares these records and the co-presidents review them. At the final General Meeting, the presidents and finance team present a closing income statement and statement of cash flows. Our team charter treats financial dishonesty or failure to maintain transparency as a serious infringement warranting multiple strikes.
