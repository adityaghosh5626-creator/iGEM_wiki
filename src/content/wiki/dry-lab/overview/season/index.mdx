## What went wrong, and what we changed

Much of what we built is the second or third version. Each card below is one design, build, test and learn loop: what we expected, what happened, how we found out and what we changed, with the evidence on the wiki. We kept the ones still open, because an honest status is part of the record.

Every card links to the week it happened on the [Dry Lab calendar](/dry-lab/overview/#petadex-project-calendar), where the same story can be followed step by step through the season.
