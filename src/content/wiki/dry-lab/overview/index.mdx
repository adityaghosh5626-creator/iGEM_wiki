## PETamon Game

Gotta degrade 'em all! You start with one enzyme, IsPETase, and a beach covered in plastic film. Win a battle to sequence a new enzyme, then lead your growing team into hotter, harsher sites to battle hardier plastic types. The game takes you on an odyssey through the environments where plastic is found: a 60°C compost heap, a recycling plant reactor, a landfill full of bags with no bonds to cut, and a final boss plastic that no known enzyme can break down. No single enzyme can clean up every plastic type on Earth, so we built PETadex to find the best enzyme for each and every application.

*Stats are made up for play, but the problem and the story are very real.*
