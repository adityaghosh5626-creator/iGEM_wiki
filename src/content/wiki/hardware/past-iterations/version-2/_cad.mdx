### Main Function(s) + Requirements

The only changes made to the requirements are adjustments to minimizing the print time and tolerances. Changes are highlighted in pink.

| Requirement                                        | Priority | Why (are we considering this)?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Minimize tolerance**<br /><br />Goal: < 0.3 mm   | Medium   | To ensure components do not slide or shake when using the bioreactor, the general tolerance should be low. <span style={{ backgroundColor: "#f8c8dc", padding: "0 0.2em" }}>However, having one larger dimension (width, for example) for holes in snap fits ensures an easier assembly process, thus the tolerance requirement is less relevant to **one dimension** of snapping components.</span><br /><br />For specific components like the vial cap or OD sensor holders—where light contamination will drastically alter results—the tolerance **must** be low. As printer accuracy is 0.1–0.5 mm, 0.3 mm is between the tolerance thresholds. |
| **Minimize printing time**<br /><br />Goal: < 6 hr | Low      | The average 3D print time for a small to medium CAD model ranges from 2–10 hours. As the bioreactor CAD leans towards the larger side of this range, being below 6 hr is a reasonable goal. This is dependent on the infill and print speed.<br /><br /><span style={{ backgroundColor: "#f8c8dc", padding: "0 0.2em" }}>**Note:** Goal is 15% infill and 115% print speed. A denser CAD model does not affect the bioreactor, while the print speed can go to an absolute safe maximum of 120%. </span>                                                                                                                                              |
